AI, within limits

An assistant that asks first and undoes cleanly

Bring your own model. Ask it about a selection while you write, or chat with the pages you have open. Whatever it writes is marked on the page, listed in History and undone in one step.

Ask about your open pages…
  • AI while you write. Select text and ask, continue a draft or summarize a page. The answer waits in a preview; nothing enters the page until you take it.
  • Nothing it does is final. Agents can't delete notes, rewrite history or touch a locked note.
  • Big changes wait for you. Large removals, new templates and bursts of edits go to your review queue.
  • One step back. Every agent session can be undone as new commits, back to exactly what you had.
  • Keys stay in your keychain. Never in the vault, and sent only to the address they were saved for.
