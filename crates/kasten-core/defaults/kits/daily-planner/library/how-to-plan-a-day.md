The Journal starts blank. If you want a planning routine, add the *Daily planner* template or write your own headings:

1. **Look back.** Glance at yesterday's page. Carry over what still matters; let the rest go.
2. **Pick three.** Write the three things that would make today good. If you do only these, the day went well.
3. **Give them time.** Put them in the schedule before meetings and messages fill it.
4. **Keep the rest out.** New ideas and asks go to the Inbox; sort them later.

In the evening, you can write what went well and what moves to tomorrow. Tag a to-do `#task` and give it a due date to see it on the Calendar and the board on the planner page.
