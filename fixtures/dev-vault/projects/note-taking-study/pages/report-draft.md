> People keep far more notes than they ever read again. We asked volunteers
> to find notes they wrote a month earlier and timed each search, and found
> that links beat folders in nine searches out of ten.

## 1. Introduction

Everyone keeps notes, and few can find them again. In practice, notes pile
up **across inboxes, folders and chats** that nobody tidies, and the one
note you need is the one you cannot find.
