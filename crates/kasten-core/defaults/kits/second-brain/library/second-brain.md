> [!tip] Four places for everything
> **Projects** have a goal and an end. **Areas** are what you look after with no end date. **Resources** are topics you're interested in. The **Archive** is everything no longer active. Sort by where you'll use a note, not by what it's about.

## Projects

Every project in the sidebar is one: its pages, cards and whiteboards live together. Start one with *New project*; when it's done, archive it from its menu.

## Archive

Archived projects stay in the sidebar under *Archived*, whole and searchable.

## Keeping it tidy

- Capture anywhere: the Inbox (Ctrl+N) takes anything.
- Once a week, move each Inbox note to a project, tag it `#area` or `#resource`, or let it go.
- When a project ends, archive it. Nothing is lost.
