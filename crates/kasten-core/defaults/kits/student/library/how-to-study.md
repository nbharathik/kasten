A few habits that make notes worth keeping:

1. **Write in your own words.** After a lecture, close the slides and write the three main ideas from memory.
2. **Ask questions.** End each lecture's notes with what you don't understand yet, and look for answers before the next one.
3. **Space it out.** Look back at a topic the next day, a week later, and a month later. A *Study plan* page helps before exams.
4. **Link ideas.** Use `[[` to connect a lecture to earlier ones and to your reading. Understanding lives in the links.
