> [!tip] How this works
> Catch thoughts as **fleeting notes**. Take **literature notes** as you read, in your own words. Once a day, turn what's worth keeping into **permanent notes**: one idea each, linked to the notes it builds on. **Maps of content** are the entry points.

## Habits that make it work

- Write the idea as a full sentence in the title.
- Link every new note to at least one older note. Use `[[` to find it.
- When a topic has a dozen notes, give it a map of content.
- Trash a fleeting note once it has become a permanent note, or once it no longer interests you.
