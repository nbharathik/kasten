Once a week, about an hour. Duplicate this page, or tick and untick.

## Get clear

- [ ] Empty the Inbox: give every item a next action, a project, or the trash
- [ ] Collect loose papers, notes and messages into the Inbox
- [ ] Clear your head: write down anything still on your mind

## Get current

- [ ] Go through *Next*: tick what's done, drop what isn't needed
- [ ] Go through *Waiting for*: nudge anyone overdue
- [ ] Look at last week's and next week's calendar
- [ ] Check every project has a next action

## Get creative

- [ ] Read *Someday*: move anything that's now worth doing to *Next*
- [ ] Any new projects or ideas?
