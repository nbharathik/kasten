How it flows

From a passing thought to a finished plan

Kasten follows an idea from the moment you catch it to the day you present it, and keeps it findable after.

  1. Capture

    Press Ctrl N anywhere and jot it down. Thoughts land in the Inbox, where a key files each one into a project, onto a board or under a tag.

  2. Organise

    Every project opens on its own home: a summary, its pages and to-dos, and its whiteboards. Drag a page onto a project to move it there.

  3. Arrange

    Drag pages onto a whiteboard as cards, connect them with arrows and gather them into sections. Sketch beside them with the pen and shapes.

  4. Present

    Press P on a board and each section becomes a slide, shown full screen. Escape brings back the view you had.

  5. Find it again

    Months later, search and the Card Library bring back any note by its words, tags, project or date.
