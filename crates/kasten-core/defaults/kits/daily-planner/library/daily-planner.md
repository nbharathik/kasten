> [!tip] How this works
> Every day starts with a blank page in the **Journal** (Ctrl+J). Write freely, or add the *Daily planner* template when you want structure. Tasks with a due date appear below and on the Calendar. Once a week, start a page from the *Weekly plan* template; once a month, a *Monthly review*.

## The rhythm

- **Each morning:** open today in the Journal and pick three things that would make the day good.
- **Each evening:** tick what's done and move the rest to tomorrow.
- **Each week:** a *Weekly plan*, then a *Weekly review* at the end of it.
- **Each month:** a *Monthly review*, and next month's goals.

Anything that comes up in between goes in the Inbox with a quick note (Ctrl+N), so the day's page stays short.
