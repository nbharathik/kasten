A project keeps everything for one piece of work together: its pages, its
quick cards and its whiteboards. On disk it is one folder, so a whole project
can be moved, shared or archived at once. This one is an example to explore,
rename, fill in or delete.

> [!info] This page is the project's home
> Write the overview here. As you add pages, cards and whiteboards, the top of
> this page gathers them, with the project's open to-dos.

## Goal

What does done look like? One or two sentences are enough, for example:
*Plan and run a small community event by the end of next month.*

## Next steps

- [ ] Write the goal above in your own words
- [ ] Add a page for each part of the work with **+** beside the project in the sidebar
- [ ] Catch ideas with **Ctrl+N**, then move them here from the **Inbox**
- [ ] Sketch how the parts fit together on a whiteboard
- [ ] Set a status and a deadline in the right panel (**Ctrl+.**)

## What goes in a project

| Kind | Good for | Kept in |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | Plans, drafts and meeting notes: anything long | `pages/` |
| Cards | Single ideas, quotes and questions | `cards/` |
| Whiteboards | Maps, flows and brainstorms | `boards/` |

## Working with projects

- **Add to project** at the top of any page moves it into a project, links and all.
- Pin a project, drag projects into your own order, or archive one when it is
  done; an archived project keeps its notes and history.
- Type `/table view` in a page to track items such as tasks or ideas, each a
  note of its own.
- Everything here is found by **Ctrl+K**, and every change is in the history.
